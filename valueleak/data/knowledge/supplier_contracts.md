# Synthetic Supplier Contracts

All information in this document is fictional and created for the ValueLeak AI demonstration.

## Contract SUP-001 — COMP-A

Contract period: 01 January 2026 - 31 December 2026

Base unit price: EUR 12.00.

### Annual volume discount

Purchases below 5,000 units:
0% discount.

Cumulative annual purchases of 5,000 units or more:
10% discount.

The threshold is calculated using cumulative quantities purchased during the calendar year.

Once the 5,000-unit threshold is reached, the 10% discount applies retroactively to all eligible COMP-A units purchased during that calendar year.

Suppliers must issue the corresponding credit or price adjustment.
