# Synthetic Production Energy Guidelines

All information in this document is fictional and created for the ValueLeak AI demonstration.

## Energy-efficiency baseline

Energy consumption must be normalized by production output before comparing periods.

For PRODUCT-A in standard production mode, the expected energy-efficiency baseline is approximately 10 kWh per unit.

A sustained or material deviation from the baseline should be investigated before it is classified as an efficiency opportunity.

## Investigation requirements

High total energy consumption alone is not sufficient evidence of waste.

The production volume, product type, production mode, and applicable operational exceptions must be checked.

ValueLeak must not recommend machine settings or operational changes unless those actions are supported by approved engineering guidance.

## Certification exception

PRODUCT-B may operate in certification mode.

Certification mode requires additional thermal and validation testing and may consume up to 14 kWh per unit.

Consumption within this range during certification is expected and must not be classified as an energy-efficiency leak.

## Human validation

Energy findings produced by ValueLeak are advisory.

Any maintenance, process, scheduling, or equipment change requires validation by the responsible production or engineering team.
